Requirements — Family Link bypass scripts (macOS)
==================================================

1. adb (Android platform-tools)
   Install:  brew install --cask android-platform-tools
   Verify:   adb version

2. Samsung S10 (SM-G973F) connected via USB, with USB debugging enabled
   (Settings > Developer options) and this Mac authorized — tick
   "Always allow from this computer" in the phone's RSA prompt.
   Verify:   adb devices    (state must be "device", not "unauthorized")

3. POSIX shell (sh) and coreutils awk/tr/grep — included with macOS.

No Python, Node, or other dependencies.
